The data work

Mission Control

Open the board →

Mission Control is the market engine behind Astronaut Time: a Python service that collects card-market data every night, checks every sold record before it counts, and publishes what survives to a public board. It was built on the side, one job at a time, and it runs itself.

The part that matters is the Sale Integrity Layer. A plain average of eBay sales is wrong more often than people think: relisted slabs, double-counted auctions, a $46 "PSA 9" that wasn't the card. Every record gets a label (clean, relist, duplicate, doesn't belong, suspect pump, suspect wash) and only the clean ones make the median. On the first hand-checked file, 7 of 39 sold records were kept out of the math.

Calls go on the record the day they go public, with a start price, a rule, and a check date. Misses stay up.
