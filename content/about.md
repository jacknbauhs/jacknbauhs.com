Jack Bauhs is a Chicago-born director, entrepreneur, sales strategist, and digital creator building at the intersection of storytelling, commerce, and culture.

After leaving Chicago to pursue film, Jack studied Broadcast Journalism and Documentary at Chapman University's Dodge College of Film and Media Arts, where he developed a strong foundation in cinematic storytelling, editing, and narrative structure.

Blending hypnagogic visuals with emotionally driven pacing and technical precision in post-production, Jack has collaborated with industry professionals including Kylie Jenner, Jeremy Irons, Wasserman Agency, Playboi Carti, Polo G, Yung Gravy, ESPN, Disney, Netflix, Amazon, Paramount Plus, and the Red Cross.

Beyond filmmaking, Jack works in enterprise technology sales at Docusign, helping organizations operate more efficiently and securely. The role complements his creative background with real-world business execution.

Jack is also the founder of Astronaut Time, a collectibles intelligence and media brand: Pokémon market media, rendered and edited, built on real data from Mission Control, the market engine he built. Every number it publishes carries its source and its date, every call is dated and checked in public, and the fake sales are taken out before the math.

At his core, Jack is a storyteller, whether through film, business, or the market.
